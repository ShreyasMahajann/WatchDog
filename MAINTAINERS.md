## What maintainers do

- Review and merge pull requests; every change needs a maintainer's approval.
- Triage issues and label them.
- Decide on releases, the backend/client split, and changes to the correlation
  engine contract.
- Handle security reports privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and
  enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How decisions are made

Small changes are decided in the pull request. Larger changes (new tools, new
platforms, new dependencies, changes to correlation semantics) start as an
issue so others can comment; the lead maintainer makes the final call and
records the reason in the issue.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who make several solid pull requests and help others in reviews and
issues may be invited to become maintainers.
